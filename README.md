LUCAS(1)                     General Commands Manual                    LUCAS(1)

NAME
       lucas - lead software engineer, 14 years in production

SYNOPSIS
       lucas [--backend] [--frontend] [--network] [--ai] [--lead] <problem>

DESCRIPTION
       Lucas Fontes Gaspareto, also answering to devgaspa. Paid to write
       software since 2012, starting in Oracle PL/SQL and Forms & Reports and
       arriving at TypeScript, NestJS and React by way of jQuery, ExtJS, Nuxt
       and a few things best left in SVN. Based in Cianorte, Parana, Brazil.
       Works remote.

       Most of that time was spent in telecom, building corporate software:
       TVA and Vivo early on, then M2M connectivity and MVNO integrations,
       and lately routers and network infrastructure at Jointer.

       Today lucas is the technical owner of a connectivity portal with 519
       endpoints and 72 modules covering ACL, M2M SIM management, eSIM,
       router management and CRM, and leads a team of five developers.

       A pattern worth knowing: lucas tends to end up building the base the
       rest of the team stands on. The screen development standard at EBDS,
       the ACL API at TecToy that is still the foundation of the portal two
       companies later, the AI development workspace at Jointer. Also learned
       the hard way what over-engineering and too many dependencies do to a
       large codebase, and now treats every new abstraction as guilty until
       proven useful.

OPTIONS
       --backend
              Node.js with NestJS. Oracle PL/SQL, still, because the data
              lives there and it is not moving. Postgres. Go when something
              needs to be small and fast.

       --frontend
              TypeScript and React. Did the Nuxt and Vue years and would not
              complain about going back. React Native and PWAs for field
              apps.

       --network
              FreeRADIUS, OpenWRT, Ansible, captive portals. Designed and
              built a system that provisions routers with captive portals
              remotely. Every bar and clinic with free Wi-Fi is quietly
              running something like this.

       --ai   Specializing in AI-assisted development with Claude Code.
              Built a harness of custom skills for the team that automates
              releases, QA and route provisioning, then used it to refactor
              the whole frontend codebase and fix legacy mistakes.

       --lead Leads a team of five. Took the Product Owner seat in 2024 as
              well: features, backlog, deadlines. Prefers to understand what
              the client actually needs before organizing it for the team.

FILES
       glamspot.com.br
              A booking platform for beauty salons. 22,000 salons catalogued,
              paying customers, built and run by one person. That person is
              lucas.

       ~/side-projects
              A countdown to Halley's comet (next visit 2061, no rush). An
              idle clicker themed on the Brazilian stock exchange. A pile of

HISTORY
       2012   Kennen. First job, huge Oracle codebase in SVN. Learned to read
              a spec and turn it into code. Studied jQuery at night.
       2015   Castrolanda. Three months writing the intake and washing
              module for a potato plant. First requirements taken directly
              from people on the factory floor.
       2016   EBDS. Oracle APEX, then a starter project that let Forms &
              Reports developers write ExtJS and Node with a small learning
              curve. Became the team standard for building screens.
       2017   Avanti. Brazilian e-commerce on VTEX, all jQuery and CSS.
       2018   BBX. A friend's dare: an isomorphic Nuxt and Firebase app. The
              product was a lingerie catalog for Hope.
       2018   EBDS again. A venture capital platform in Nuxt and Firebase,
              then React and React Native field apps on top of a telecom CRM
              with a PL/SQL backend.
       2022   TecToy. An MDM proof of concept, and the ACL API: KrakenD
              gateway, RBAC and ABAC, API keys, the works.
       2023   Ettera. Lead engineer on an M2M connectivity portal. Designed
              the layer that unifies several MVNO providers under one API.
              Migrated Postgres to Oracle and AWS to Oracle OCI.
       2025   Jointer. Lead engineer. Merged two platforms into a single
              multi-brand codebase without stopping either product in
              production.
